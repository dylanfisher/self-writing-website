What is this?

A web page that anyone can change. Leave a suggestion and an AI edits the page to follow it, one suggestion at a time, in the order they came in. Every version is kept, so you can scrub back through all of them.

The AI is Qwen, an open model running locally on a MacBook Pro in my closet. No cloud AI involved. You can watch it write each change live under “Now writing”.

The model has no tool calls and no internet access. All it sees is the current page and the suggestions, and all it can do is write edits to the page, which then runs sealed off in your browser with no network.

It makes small edits rather than rewriting the whole page, and it remembers your last few suggestions, so you can follow up with “no, bigger” or “on all four sides”.

        What are rules?
        
            
        Most suggestions change the page once. A rule is a suggestion that keeps applying: ask for something
              to happen on every change, like “update the title on every edit” or “each time, add a star”, and the
              AI turns it into a rule.
        
            
        Every change after that follows the rules too, on top of its own suggestion. If the two clash, the
              suggestion wins that time.
        
            
        Up to 5 rules are active at once, for everyone. A new rule pushes
              out the oldest. To end one early, suggest “stop …”, or remove your own with Remove.
